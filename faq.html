Frequently Asked Questions

How does Zodimithra work?

Zodimithra blends classical Vedic astrology with refined intelligence. Once you share your birth details, we generate a personalized chart, daily readings, and contextual insights tailored to your stars.

Is my data private?

Always. Your birth details and chart are encrypted, never sold, and used only to craft your personal experience.

Do I need any astrology knowledge to begin?

Not at all. Zodimithra is designed for newcomers and seasoned seekers alike — readings are presented clearly, with optional depth for those who want more.

What traditions does Zodimithra follow?

Primarily Vedic (sidereal) astrology, with attention to Nakshatra, Dasha, and classical principles. Western tools are available where useful.

Is the app free?

The core experience is free. Premium readings — birth chart deep-dives, compatibility, muhurta — are part of a subtle, transparent subscription.

Can I use Zodimithra on the web?

Yes. You can read insights and blogs here, while the full personalized companion lives in the mobile app.

How accurate are the readings?

Accuracy depends on precise birth time and place. With those, our readings closely follow classical Vedic methodologies reviewed by traditional scholars.
